## Abstract

Large language models are powerful but costly to run. This work proposes a hybrid Green AI framework that combines pruning with INT8 quantization on DistilBERT, and measures the real energy cost with CodeCarbon instead of estimating it. The aim is a model that stays accurate while using far less energy and memory.
