## Abstract

Early detection saves lives, but screening is hard where specialists are few. This work builds a multimodal screening model that combines tabular data (LightGBM, XGBoost) with images (EfficientNet-B0), explains its decisions with SHAP, and exports to ONNX so it can run on a phone inside PustiPath.
