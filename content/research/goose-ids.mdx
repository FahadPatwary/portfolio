## Abstract

GOOSE messages carry time-critical protection signals inside power substations, so a detector must be both accurate and extremely fast. This work presents a two-stage anomaly detection pipeline for GOOSE traffic in IEC 61850 smart-grid networks, built around a One-Class SVM.
